Schuylkill Intermediate Unit 29
What We Do

IU29 is the regional backbone for STEM education across Schuylkill County — connecting schools with resources, mentors, and programs that no single district could build alone.

Makerspace Development
We help schools design, fund, and launch makerspaces — from laser cutters and 3D printers to CNC routers and electronics labs. Your kid's school can have one too.
STEM Competitions
From the PA Keystone STEM Competition to the Sea Air & Land Challenge, we run county-wide events where students compete, collaborate, and showcase real-world skills to real industry judges.
Industry Connections
We recruit retired machinists, engineers, and tech professionals from across the coal region to mentor students — and connect local manufacturers to the next-generation workforce they need.

Get Involved

Whether you're a teacher with a big idea or a community member with hard-earned skills, there's a place for you in the Schuylkill STEM ecosystem.

For Teachers & Administrators
Pitch a Project

Have an idea for a makerspace, lab, or STEM program? Submit a pitch and IU29 will help you find equipment, funding, and mentors to make it real — at no cost to you.

  • Makerspace equipment requests
  • STEM lab design & layout support
  • Competition team setup & coaching
  • AI & computational thinking curriculum
For Community Members

Retired? Still sharp? The coal region built things for generations. We need those skills back in our schools. Join our mentor network and help students build the next chapter.

  • Retired machinists & welders
  • Engineers & industrial designers
  • Coal & energy sector veterans
  • Tech & coding professionals
  • Business & entrepreneurship mentors

Building the Ecosystem Together
Our Partners

STEM education doesn't happen in a vacuum. These organizations help connect students to real careers, real tools, and real futures right here in the coal region.

Manufacturing & Industry
Schuylkill County Manufacturers
Regional manufacturers providing equipment donations, facility tours, and professional mentors to connect classrooms to the shop floor and real career pathways.
Higher Education
Penn State Schuylkill & Regional Colleges
Dual enrollment pathways, campus visits, and college readiness programming connecting IU29 students to postsecondary STEM opportunities without leaving the county.
Workforce Development
Schuylkill Chamber & Economic Dev
Connecting workforce pipeline data with classroom programming — making sure what students learn today matches what Schuylkill County employers actually need tomorrow.
